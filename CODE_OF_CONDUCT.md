# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about diagrams, not
about people.

## The longer version

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant][cc]. In practice,
that means:

**Expected:** patience with people who are new to any part of this — agents,
diagramming, or open source. Clear, specific feedback. Crediting other people's
work, including every icon library author whose set makes this useful.

**Not welcome:** harassment of any kind, personal attacks, demeaning comments
about someone's tooling or experience level, publishing anyone's private
information, or sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **mouadpro02@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.
Depending on severity, the response ranges from a private word to a permanent
ban from the project's spaces. Whoever handles a report will recuse themselves
if they are involved in it.

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
